# Shot list

## How to shoot (all three houses)
- Use 1x on the iPhone, not 0.5x. The ultra-wide is what makes the current photos bend and lean.
- Phone at chest height, held level. Turn on Settings > Camera > Grid and keep the door frames upright.
- Shoot in the day with the shutters open, then again at dusk with every lamp on. Dusk is where these houses look best.
- Take landscape and portrait of each shot.
- No people, no bags, no chargers, no clutter.
- Horizontal shots get the wide slots on the site. Vertical ones sit beside them.
